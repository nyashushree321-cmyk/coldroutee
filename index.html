The problem

Perishables don't forgive a warm truck.

Anyone shipping cakes, dairy, seafood or frozen food knows the same three headaches. ColdRoute is built around fixing each one.

Finding a reefer you can trust

Dry-truck apps can't carry perishables. Here every vehicle is verified: registration, permit, insurance, licence, and hygiene and pest-control certificates. If a hygiene certificate lapses, the truck is blocked from cold jobs automatically.

Proving it stayed cold

When cargo arrives warm, receivers can claim damage or charge cooling fees. Continuous temperature logs, door-open events and the temperature at handover become the evidence that protects both shipper and carrier.

Mixing loads safely

Fish shouldn't share air with ice cream. Veg and non-veg stay apart. Pharma is isolated. A rules engine checks every load, and operations can edit the rules without waiting for a software release.

Not a general freight app

ColdRoute is an end-to-end, on-demand cold-chain marketplace. Dry-truck platforms such as Porter mainly move dry, ambient, local cargo.

    Can these ride together?

    Pick two cargo types. Rules follow the project's initial segregation set, to be confirmed with operations and quality staff.

    Vehicle typesReefer trucks, vans and multi-temperature vehicles, plus dry-cargo vehicles for loads that aren't temperature-sensitive.
    Every compartment recordsPosition (front, middle, rear) and loading sequence · capacity by weight, volume and pallets · temperature range and setpoint · independent or shared cooling · fixed or movable bulkhead · linked sensors.
    Hybrid loadsA food chain can send dry bakery supplies and frozen inventory in a single run: dry goods ambient, frozen goods at −18 °C.

    The segregation rules

    PharmaDedicated compartment or vehicle. Never shared with commercial foods or odour-producing goods.
    Veg vs non-vegVegetarian and Jain foods stay strictly apart from non-veg: separate compartments, or separate vehicles for strict dietary or religious needs.
    Meat and seafoodNot combined with dairy, bakery or ice cream unless sealed packaging and a separate compartment are validated.
    Smell transferStrong-odour cargo is flagged and isolated so its fumes can't reach ice cream, dairy and cakes.
    Conflicting temperaturesCombined only where the vehicle has validated compartments for each band.
    AllergensFlagged per consignment. Conflicting allergen loads aren't combined without an approved policy.

    Initial rule set, to be confirmed by operations and quality staff. Rules live in admin-editable configuration, so they can be tightened or relaxed without a software release.

      Questions

      Good to know

      Is this like Porter?

      Similar booking experience, different job. Porter moves ordinary goods, mostly inside a city. ColdRoute is for temperature-controlled goods over longer distances, with temperature monitoring and proof built in.

      Can I ship medicines?

      Pharma is planned but gated. It opens only after licensing, packaging and quality validation are in place, and it always travels in its own compartment or vehicle.

      What if cargo arrives warm?

      The platform records continuous temperature, door openings and the handover reading. That record is the evidence for resolving a dispute fairly. Thresholds and compensation rules per cargo type are still to be agreed.

      Which cities will launch first?

      Pilot corridors are still to be decided. We expand one corridor at a time as the data supports it.

      I own reefer trucks. Can I join?

      Yes: as an owner-driver, a fleet owner or an aggregator. Join the early-access list and tell us your vehicle count and routes.

      Which tracking hardware do I need?

      Phase 1 supports driver-entered temperature readings plus one integrated telematics vendor (GeoTracker or an equivalent). Whether hardware is mandatory for every vehicle is still being decided.

      What do stop labels like A1 and B2 mean?

      Each stop gets a label so everyone means the same stop. The working assumption: the letter identifies the consignment or customer (A, B) and the number is the stop sequence, so A1 is a pickup and A2 its drop. This is to be confirmed.

      Who issues the GST invoice?

      The platform starts as a marketplace and transport coordinator. Carrier, transporter and GST invoicing roles, including whether an aggregator issues the invoice, are being confirmed with Indian legal and tax advisers.

      What is still being decided?

      Open items with the client: the stop-label convention; carrier of record and GST invoicing; pharma in the launch scope and the licences it needs; who supplies telematics hardware; excursion thresholds and compensation rules per cargo category; how strict veg and non-veg separation must be; the first pilot corridors and city clusters; and how long telemetry is kept for audits and disputes.
