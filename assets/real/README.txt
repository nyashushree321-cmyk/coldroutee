Put your REAL truck media in this folder. The website picks the files up automatically.

HERO (top of the page) - the site uses the first of these that exists:
  1. hero-truck.mp4   A real clip of a refrigerated truck moving. H.264 MP4, no sound needed,
                      landscape 16:9, about 8-15 seconds, ideally under 8 MB.
  2. hero-truck.jpg   A real photo (jpg, jpeg, webp or png). It gets a slow camera move so it feels alive.
                      Landscape, at least 1600 px wide, with the truck on the right half of the picture,
                      because the headline sits on the left.
  3. If neither exists, the animated clip (assets/hero.mp4) plays.

PHOTO GALLERY ("On the road" section)
  truck-1.jpg ... truck-6.jpg   Real photos of trucks (jpg, jpeg, webp or png).
                      The section stays hidden until at least one photo exists.

Only use photos and video you own or are licensed to use.
